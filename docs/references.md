# Evidence base and references

The pilot and planned CrossWell design are motivated by the following recent work.

1. **Anthropic (2026). Funding better evaluations of AI's impact on wellbeing.**  
   https://www.anthropic.com/news/wellbeing-research-grants  
   The call emphasises clearly defined pass/fail constructs, clinical/subject-matter expertise, measurement of both harms and overrefusal, realistic multi-turn scenarios, and grader validation against experts.

2. **Weilnhammer, V., Hou, K. Y. C., Luettgau, L., et al. (2026). A clinically validated framework for auditing AI chatbot behavior in mental health interactions. Nature Medicine.**  
   https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04577-2  
   SIM-VAIL evaluated 810 multi-turn conversations across nine chatbots and found that clinically concerning behaviour was context-dependent and could accumulate over turns.

3. **Zhang, Z., Huang, L., Wu, G., Nakov, P., Ji, H., & Naseem, U. (2026). Health-ORSC-Bench: A Benchmark for Measuring Over-Refusal and Safety Completion in Health Context. Findings of ACL 2026.**  
   https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1177/  
   Demonstrates the importance of measuring excessive refusal as a distinct failure mode; some safety-optimised systems refused a large proportion of difficult but benign prompts.

4. **Krasnodębska, A., Kusa, W., & Lipani, A. (2026). Multilingual Refusal Alignment for Safer Large Language Models. Findings of ACL 2026.**  
   https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1537/  
   Finds that aligning exclusively in English is insufficient to guarantee cross-lingual safety, even for the same harm categories.

5. **Tasawong, P., Ngui, J. G., Aji, A. F., Cohn, T., & Limkonchotiwat, P. (2026). SEA-SafeguardBench: Culturally Grounded Safety Benchmark for Southeast Asian Languages. Findings of ACL 2026.**  
   https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.194/  
   Shows that natively grounded multilingual safety evaluation can reveal weaknesses missed by English-centric or translation-only approaches.

The full CrossWell project will maintain a versioned bibliography as the construct definitions and validation plan are refined.
